Completed
ClientBBC Studios / Production team
Vantor's roleScenic build, bar installation & crew coordination
Crew suppliedScenic carpenters, build crew, crew boss oversight
Year2025
StatusDelivered on time, to BBC production spec

BBC Radio 1 Big Weekend is one of the UK's largest annual music events — a multi-stage, multi-day production run to the standards of a national broadcaster. Vantor supplied scenic carpenters and build crew for bar construction and structural scenic installs across multiple stage areas.

The job demanded crew who understood a broadcast production environment — call times, site hierarchy, H&S documentation and the pace that a BBC production schedule requires. Every crew member was briefed ahead of site, RAMS were issued, and the build was delivered without delay.

This is the kind of job that tells you whether a crew supplier is the real thing. We were rebooked.

Completed
Vantor's roleFull festival crew cycle — load-in to strike
Crew suppliedFestival crew, scenic carpenters, crew boss
DurationMulti-day build, event days & full de-rig
Year2025

BBC Radio 2 in the Park required a complete crew deployment from load-in through to strike. Vantor managed the full cycle — structural builds, scenic installs, event-day operations and a clean, on-schedule de-rig.

Multi-day festival builds test a crew supplier in ways single-day jobs don't. Managing crew energy and performance over three or four days, maintaining safety standards when everyone is tired, and delivering a clean site handover — these are the measures that matter. We delivered to spec across every phase.

Exhibition · Corporate

Exhibition & Trade Show Builds

Ongoing
VenuesExCeL London · Manchester Central · NEC Birmingham
Crew suppliedScenic carpenters, build crew, exhibition installers
SectorsTrade exhibitions, brand activations, product launches

Exhibition builds have their own specific demands — typically tight load-in windows, high finishing standards and clients who will be standing in their stands the moment the doors open. Every exhibitor expects their stand to be complete, clean and exactly as specified.

Vantor has supplied scenic carpenters and build crew across major UK exhibition venues. Our crew understand that an exhibition build isn't complete when it's structurally done — it's complete when it looks right. That attention to finish is what gets you rebooked by exhibition contractors.

TV & Broadcast · London

Television Set Builds & Broadcast Productions

Ongoing
Crew suppliedScenic carpenters, set builders, crew bosses
StandardsBroadcast-grade finishing · Tight schedule compliance
LocationLondon studios & broadcast facilities

Broadcast set builds operate to a different standard to most construction work. The timelines are measured in hours, not days. The finish has to be camera-ready. And there is no room for crew who need things explained twice.

Vantor has supplied scenic carpenters to TV and broadcast productions across London. Harry's own background spans the BAFTAs, Britain's Got Talent, Dancing on Ice and ITV productions — that experience directly informs the standard our carpenters are held to.
