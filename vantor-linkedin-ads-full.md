## PART 3 — SINGLE IMAGE vs CAROUSEL

### Single Image Ad Specs & Direction

**Format:** 1200 × 627px (1.91:1) or 1200 × 1200px (square — better feed real estate on mobile)

**Recommended image direction (3 options):**
1. **Hero shot:** Crew in branded Vantor hi-vis/black tees mid load-in at a recognisable venue type (truss going up, flight cases rolling in). Real, not stock.
2. **Crew chief portrait:** Confident shot of a crew chief on radio, clipboard in hand, action blurred behind. Humanises the brand.
3. **Bold typographic:** Black background, white text — *"6am. Truck's here. You're two crew down."* Vantor logo bottom right. Works hard on scroll-stop.

**On-image text overlay (keep under 20% of image area):**
- "Event Crew. London & Manchester."
- "vantorltd.com"

Pair any of the three with SC Ad A, B, or C above.

## PART 5 — LEAD-GEN FORM COPY

Use this for **both** the on-site landing page form *and* LinkedIn's native Lead Gen Form (which auto-fills from user profiles — far higher conversion than off-platform).

### Form Headline
Get a crew quote inside the working day.

### Form Intro / Description
Tell us about your event and we'll come back with availability and a transparent quote — usually within a few hours. No obligation, no sales pressure.

### Form Fields (keep it short — 5 max for best conversion)
1. **Full name*** (auto-fill on LinkedIn)
2. **Work email*** (auto-fill)
3. **Company*** (auto-fill)
4. **Event location*** — dropdown: London / Manchester / Other UK / International
5. **Event date & crew needed*** — open text field, placeholder: *"e.g. 14–16 Oct, 6 crew + chief, conference load-in/out"*

*(Optional 6th field if you want richer leads: **Phone number** — pre-filled where available.)*
